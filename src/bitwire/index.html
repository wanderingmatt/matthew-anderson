Bitwire

Bitwire makes it easy to write press releases in one place and distribute them anywhere.

I joined Bitwire via contract when all they had was initial wireframes and a vision. Over the course of a year, I ran product, designed and shipped their MVP, established their brand, built their design system, and helped navigate their first post-launch iterations.

Product

  • Product Manager
  • Principal Designer

Bitwire had been working on their MVP for several months without shipping anything. The team had solid ambition but was struggling to define exactly what they were building. They reached out for help—I took over product immediately, leading the development agency they'd been working with and working directly with the CEO to get things unstuck. The result was a shipped MVP that got Bitwire to market and into the hands of their first customers.

Website

  • Product Manager
  • Principal Designer
  • Front-End Developer

While the team worked toward product-market fit, the website needed to pull its weight — self-serving prospective customers and educating them about the product without requiring a sales call. I designed and built a site that communicated what Bitwire does clearly and quickly, giving the business room to iterate on positioning as they learned more about their market. The site was developed with Webflow so that anyone on the team could update it after I left.

Design System

  • Product Manager
  • Principal Designer

I built the Bitwire design system on top of Material Design — a deliberate choice that made the system easier for future contractors to pick up and maintain without needing to onboard deeply. On top of that foundation I layered custom components specific to Bitwire's brand, including colored gradients that gave the product a distinct personality within a familiar framework.
