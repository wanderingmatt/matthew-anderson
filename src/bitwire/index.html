Design System

I built the Bitwire design system on top of Material Design — a deliberate choice that made the system easier for future contractors to pick up and maintain without needing to onboard deeply. On top of that foundation I layered custom components specific to Bitwire's brand, including colored gradients that gave the product a distinct personality within a familiar framework.
