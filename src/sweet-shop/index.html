Sweet Shop

{ "project": "sweet-shop", "alt": "Sweet Shop – Key Image" }

A digital comics store that believes there is a comic for everyone.

I co-founded Sweet with the mission to grow comics through heartfelt technology. Our flagship product is a digital comics store designed to help everyone discover, read, and share comics without commoditizing creators.

App

  • Design Director
  • Principal Designer

Comics are one of the most creative and expressive storytelling mediums on the planet. The most fun and humbling challenge of designing Sweet Shop was getting out of the way—letting the art of creators shine through while still wrapping everything in the best possible UX. Artwork sizes, description lengths, and metadata vary wildly across books and publishers, so every decision had to account for that.

Brand

  • Design Director
  • Designed by Jez Burrows

I wanted a brand that could hold its own alongside the art it would be presenting—something that felt like comics themselves:

  • Joyful
  • Expressive
  • Quirky

The logo bridges the gap between the physical world and the digital one we're building. The sign is a nod to the experience of discovering your favorite comic shop from the sidewalk and the swirls tie it back to Sweet with a wink toward old-timey candy patterns. Meanwhile, the face turns the logo into a character in its own right, providing us with opportunities to experiment with expression in a way you can only do in digital form. I incorporated the logo into a brand as colorful as comics.
