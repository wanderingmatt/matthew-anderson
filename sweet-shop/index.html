Sweet Shop

A digital comics store that believes there is a comic for everyone.

I co-founded Sweet with the mission to grow comics through heartfelt technology. Our flagship product is designed to help anyone discover, read, and share comics without commoditizing creators.

App

  • Design Director
  • Principal Designer

Comics are one of the most creative and expressive storytelling mediums on the planet. The most fun and humbling challenge of designing Sweet Shop was getting out of the way—letting the art shine through while wrapping everything in the best possible UX. Artwork sizes, description lengths, and metadata vary wildly across books and publishers, so every decision had to account for that.

Brand & Design System

  • Design Director
  • Designed by Jez Burrows

I wanted a brand that could hold its own alongside the art it would be presenting. Something that bridged the gap between the physical world of print and the digital one we're creating. I wanted it to feel:

  • Joyful
  • Expressive
  • Quirky

The sign is a nod to the experience of discovering your favorite comic shop from the sidewalk; the swirls tie it back to Sweet with a wink toward old-timey candy patterns. Meanwhile, the face turns the logo into a character in its own right, providing us with opportunities to experiment with expression in a way you can only do in digital form.

I incorporated the logo into a vibrant, colorful brand, supported by a bespoke design system built to mode-switch between multiple themes. The system is instructed via agentic skills through Figma MCP and backed by design tokens synced to Supernova.
